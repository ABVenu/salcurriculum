Session Title: Understanding break, continue, and Nested Loops

Scene 1.0: Break and Continue
LO 1: Understand the purpose of the break statement in exiting loops.
LO 2: Demonstrate the use of the break statement within loops to control loop execution.
LO 3:Explain how to use the break statement in a while loop combined with an if condition.
LO 4:Identify the functionality of the continue statement in loops.
LO 5:Apply the continue statement to skip specific iterations in a loop based on a condition.
Scene 2.0: Nested Loops
LO 1: Understand the need for nested loops with real-life examples, such as an alarm clock.
LO 2: Identify the syntax of nested loops and how outer and inner loops work together.
LO 3:Demonstrate the use of start, stop, and step with nested loops to control iterations.
LO 4: Apply nested loops to solve problems by iterating over multiple ranges.
LO 5:Create practical examples of nested loops, such as the field and seed example, to reinforce understanding.
