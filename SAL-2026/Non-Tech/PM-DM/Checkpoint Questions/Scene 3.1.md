#### **Scene 3.1**

1. **You asked for a short checklist to prepare for an offline viva. The model returns a long essay comparing foreign universities. What failed first?**  
   a) Tokenization — the model ran out of tokens and padded the essay  
   b) Relevance — it answered a nearby question and ignored length  
   c) Face matching — the model could not recognise the user’s photograph  
   d) Retrieval — it fetched your viva marks from the exam database  

   **Answer:** b) Relevance — it answered a nearby question and ignored length  

   **Explanation:** Relevance asks whether the reply answers *this* request, keeps the audience, and respects constraints such as length. A fluent neighbouring essay can still be the wrong job.

2. **Which task is generally well-suited for Generative AI if you supply the facts?**  
   a) Choosing a medical dosage to take without consulting a doctor  
   b) Reporting this month’s attendance without opening the official portal  
   c) Submitting a graded essay as yours when generated text is forbidden  
   d) Turning points you provided into a first-draft meeting agenda  

   **Answer:** d) Turning points you provided into a first-draft meeting agenda  

   **Explanation:** First drafts from facts you supply are a strong use. Medical decisions, official records you do not have, and forbidden graded submissions are unsuitable.

3. **A draft is fluent but includes a stereotype about a community and a statistic you cannot verify. What should you do?**  
   a) Send it unchanged, because fluency means the content is fit to use  
   b) Add extra stereotypes so the paragraph looks balanced and complete  
   c) Cut the bias, drop unverified claims, and edit the draft in your voice  
   d) Paste identity documents so the model can “correct” the unverified claim  

   **Answer:** c) Cut the bias, drop unverified claims, and edit the draft in your voice  

   **Explanation:** Fitness covers safety, appropriateness, and usable voice. Remove bias, drop or mark claims you cannot check, keep private data out of the chat, and make the text sound like you before you send it.
