topics be covered: Generate multiple content drafts, such as an email, social post, or product description, using AI; Edit AI-generated copy to match a specific tone and audience; Identify common pitfalls of AI-written content, such as genericness or factual errors

detailed subtopics to be covered:
Subtopic/Scene 1: Generating Multi-Format Drafts with AI | Duration: 70 minutes
LO:1 Construct goal-driven prompts to generate diverse content formats, including promotional emails, social media updates, and product listings.
LO:2 Prompt AI to adapt message structure based on channel-specific constraints (e.g., character limits, formatting style, call-to-action placement).
LO:3 Use iterative prompt chains to quickly generate multiple creative variations for a single campaign asset.

Subtopic/Scene 2: Editing Tone, Audience Fit, and AI Copy Pitfalls | Duration: 60 minutes
LO:1 Set voice and tone guidelines, then apply human-in-the-loop edits so the draft matches a specific reader persona, using AI only to adjust complexity, length, and emphasis without changing the core message.
LO:2 Spot common AI writing flags, including repetitive buzzwords, bland jargon, formulaic structure, generic claims, and invented facts.
LO:3 Run a structured edit and fact-check on claims, links, and product details, then add real examples and human nuance before the copy is published.
