#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **A nurture email sequence has strong open rates but every email repeats essentially the same product argument. What is the primary strategic weakness?**  
   a) The sequence is not progressively resolving different customer questions over time  
   b) The sequence requires more subject-line variation before customer behaviour can improve  
   c) The sequence is likely too short because repeated arguments need greater message frequency  
   d) The sequence should move product information exclusively into sales conversations instead  

   **Answer:** a) The sequence is not progressively resolving different customer questions over time  

   **Explanation:** Nurture should move from problem relevance through education, differentiation, proof, and action—not repeat the same pitch.

2. **Which AI instruction is most likely to improve a call script for an enterprise seller?**  
   a) "Make the script persuasive and include detailed information about every product capability."  
   b) "Create a short opening, discovery questions, objection routes and one logical next step."  
   c) "Convert the existing marketing email into spoken language while retaining its full argument."  
   d) "Write a complete conversation that the seller can read without deviating from the wording."  

   **Answer:** b) "Create a short opening, discovery questions, objection routes and one logical next step."  

   **Explanation:** A call script is a conversation framework, not a monologue to read word for word.

3. **AI creates technically accurate copy that feels inappropriate for the channel. Which input was most likely under-specified?**  
   a) The communication context and behaviour expected within the selected channel  
   b) The number of alternative versions that should appear in the initial generation  
   c) The amount of competitor information included before the writing task begins  
   d) The level of vocabulary variation required between consecutive copy alternatives  

   **Answer:** a) The communication context and behaviour expected within the selected channel  

   **Explanation:** Prompts need channel, format, audience, objective, stage, and constraints—not only topic and word count.
