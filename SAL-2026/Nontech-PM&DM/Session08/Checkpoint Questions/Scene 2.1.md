#### **Scene 2.1**

1. **A company wants every marketer to apply the same 18 brand-writing rules across hundreds of recurring content tasks. Which approach is most appropriate?**  
   a) Store the reusable workflow in a Skill and provide task-specific context separately  
   b) Rewrite the full instructions manually whenever an individual content request begins  
   c) Connect additional external applications so Claude can infer the desired writing style  
   d) Reduce the guidelines to one tone adjective so marketers retain maximum flexibility  

   **Answer:** a) Store the reusable workflow in a Skill and provide task-specific context separately  

   **Explanation:** A Skill encodes repeatable operating rules; prompts handle one-off or varying task context.

2. **An AI draft has excellent tone and structure but contains an unverified claim that the product is "India's fastest platform." What should happen first?**  
   a) Retain the claim temporarily and improve its wording before external verification begins  
   b) Replace "fastest" with "leading" because softer adjectives require less supporting evidence  
   c) Verify the claim against an authoritative source or remove it from the communication  
   d) Ask a second AI model whether the statement appears commercially plausible before publishing  

   **Answer:** c) Verify the claim against an authoritative source or remove it from the communication  

   **Explanation:** Editing starts with what is true—unsupported superlatives must be verified or cut, not softened.

3. **A marketer asks AI to shorten a 200-word executive email to 80 words. The result loses the most important evidence. What was missing?**  
   a) An instruction defining which communication elements must survive the compression process  
   b) An instruction requiring greater vocabulary variation across the shortened executive message  
   c) An instruction forcing every original sentence to retain a corresponding shortened sentence  
   d) An instruction preventing the model from changing the paragraph order during the rewrite  

   **Answer:** a) An instruction defining which communication elements must survive the compression process  

   **Explanation:** When changing length, specify what must stay (proof, CTA, core message) so compression does not drop essentials.

4. **What is the strongest distinction between a Claude Skill and a connector?**  
   a) A Skill encodes repeatable ways of working, while a connector provides access to tools or data  
   b) A Skill retrieves external company information, while a connector determines editorial tone  
   c) A Skill is intended for B2B workflows, while connectors primarily support consumer marketing  
   d) A Skill creates final marketing assets, while connectors are limited to reading static documents  

   **Answer:** a) A Skill encodes repeatable ways of working, while a connector provides access to tools or data  

   **Explanation:** Skills tell Claude how to work; connectors reach where information and tools live.

5. **A draft feels recognisably "AI-written" despite having no factual errors. Which editing approach is strongest?**  
   a) Remove visible stylistic patterns while preserving generic claims and overall sentence structure  
   b) Add more unusual vocabulary so the writing differs from common generative-AI vocabulary  
   c) Re-anchor the copy in specific audience context, natural phrasing and concrete evidence  
   d) Replace all punctuation associated with AI writing before evaluating the underlying argument  

   **Answer:** c) Re-anchor the copy in specific audience context, natural phrasing and concrete evidence  

   **Explanation:** Human nuance—real examples, audience fit, and proprietary context—beats surface synonym swaps.
