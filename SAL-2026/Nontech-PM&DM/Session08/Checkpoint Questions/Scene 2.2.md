#### **Scene 2.2**

1. **A marketing team has excellent prompts but keeps receiving outdated product information in final copy. Which improvement addresses the root issue most directly?**  
   a) Increase the number of creative variants generated before selecting the final marketing draft  
   b) Ground the workflow in current approved product sources before the copy-generation step  
   c) Add more detailed tone instructions so outdated information becomes easier to identify visually  
   d) Build a longer editorial Skill that rewrites unsupported product information more conservatively  

   **Answer:** b) Ground the workflow in current approved product sources before the copy-generation step  

   **Explanation:** Connectors and approved sources should feed current facts into the workflow before drafting.

2. **A brand Skill consistently produces clear copy, but every campaign begins to sound almost identical. What is the strongest response?**  
   a) Remove all brand guidance so the model can independently create a new voice each time  
   b) Keep stable brand rules while allowing creative direction to vary by audience and objective  
   c) Add more mandatory sentence templates so variation happens inside a controlled structure  
   d) Replace the Skill with a connector because repetitive writing primarily reflects missing data  

   **Answer:** b) Keep stable brand rules while allowing creative direction to vary by audience and objective  

   **Explanation:** Standardise how you work, not every creative angle—vary brief by audience and campaign goal.

3. **Which workflow best represents effective human-in-the-loop AI content production?**  
   a) AI generates → AI scores → highest score publishes automatically → performance is reviewed later  
   b) Human briefs → AI drafts → evidence is checked → human edits → audience response validates  
   c) Human writes → AI corrects grammar → original draft is restored → marketing team publishes  
   d) AI researches → AI writes → AI fact-checks itself → human approves formatting before release  

   **Answer:** b) Human briefs → AI drafts → evidence is checked → human edits → audience response validates  

   **Explanation:** The stack ends with human judgment: brief, draft, verify, edit, then publish or test.
