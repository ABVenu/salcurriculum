Reviewed. Two changes from finance:

1. DP incentive: approved at ₹30/order. The ₹200 daily bonus is approved
   only at 10+ orders (not 8+) — at 8+ the cost goes to ~₹2.9 Cr, over
   budget. With 10+ we land at ~₹2.2 Cr.

2. Rain fee: we cannot charge ₹25 flat to everyone. Swiggy One members
   have "no surge fee" in their benefit terms. So:
     - Non-members: ₹20 (not ₹25 — leadership asked to stay below
       Zomato's rain fee for Chennai this season)
     - Swiggy One members: ₹0 (waived)
   This also means the fee offset is lower — roughly ₹0.9 Cr, not ₹1.65 Cr.
