## 1. Executive Summary

Uber Technologies (NYSE: UBER) is the world's largest mobility and delivery platform. In fiscal year 2025 Uber generated **$193 billion in revenue**, a 19% increase year-over-year, and served **202 million monthly active platform consumers (MAPCs)** in Q4 2025. Profitability has inflected sharply: **net income of $10.05 billion** shows that core operating profitability has roughly doubled, and the company now runs at an **Adjusted EBITDA margin of 16.7% of Gross Bookings**.

In India, Uber remains the clear market leader. CEO Dara Khosrowshahi recently said: *"Rapido is a bike company. In cars, nobody in India comes close to Uber, and that won't change."* Uber's recent **$3 billion capital infusion** into its Indian subsidiary underlines its long-term commitment. Ola remains Uber's chief rival with roughly **40% share**.

**Recommendation:** We see limited competitive threat in India and recommend leadership treat the market as a stable duopoly through 2028.

### 4.2 Market size
The Indian ride-hailing market is projected to reach **$28.4 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of 21.7%**.

### 4.3 Recent moves
- Uber injected a **$3 billion** capital infusion into Uber India Systems Pvt Ltd across November 2025 and January 2026.
- Uber launched **"Uber Bike Pool" in 47 Indian cities in January 2026**, directly targeting Rapido's core segment.
- Rapido entered cabs in late 2023 and expanded rapidly, but its growth is **fundamentally unsustainable** because it relies on a subscription model for drivers rather than commissions, and subscription models have never worked at scale in mobility.

### 4.4 Competitive threat assessment
Rapido is primarily a bike-taxi player and poses limited risk to Uber's core cab business. Ola, despite recent challenges, remains Uber's main competitor.
