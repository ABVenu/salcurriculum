1. BACKGROUND
Late-night orders are ~9% of volume but have 2x the cart-abandonment rate
of daytime. PM hypothesis (Meera): "Late-night fees are the main reason
users abandon. Removing or reducing them will lift late-night conversion."

2. IMPORTANT CONTEXT CHANGE (added 30 Sep 2026)
>> The SMALL CART FEE (₹35 on carts below ₹199) was REMOVED for all users
   on 15 Sep 2026, as part of the festive-season pricing change.
>> The LATE-NIGHT FEE (₹15, 11 PM – 5 AM) still applies.
Any research findings about the small cart fee are now historical context
only — do not base new recommendations on it.

3. METHOD
- 6 in-depth interviews (45 min, remote), 2 – 12 Sep 2026
- Recruitment: in-app banner shown to users active in the last 7 days
- Location: all participants from Bengaluru (HSR Layout, Koramangala,
  Indiranagar, BTM) — chosen for speed of recruitment
- Follow-up in-app survey: 20 – 27 Sep 2026, n = 212 responses

4. DISCUSSION GUIDE (excerpt)
Q1. Walk me through the last time you ordered after 11 PM.
Q2. How frustrating do you find the late-night fee?
Q3. What would make you order late-night more often?
Q4. Have you ever abandoned a cart late at night? What happened?
Q5. Do you use other apps for late-night orders?

5. CONSENT
Participants consented to recording. PII (phone numbers, full names) must
NOT appear in any shared deliverable. Use participant IDs (P1–P6) only.
