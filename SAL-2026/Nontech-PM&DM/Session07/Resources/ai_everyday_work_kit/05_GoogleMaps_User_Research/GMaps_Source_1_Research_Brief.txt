Study: "Two-wheeler riders: why they abandon navigation mid-trip"

Brief written: 28 Aug 2026   |   Last updated: 30 Sep 2026
TEACHING NOTE: FICTIONAL classroom dataset. Not real Google research. All

1. BACKGROUND
Two-wheeler (2W) mode in India has ~2x the mid-trip navigation abandonment
of car mode (rider exits navigation before reaching destination).
PM hypothesis (Meera): "Battery drain is the main reason riders quit
navigation. A low-power rider mode will lift trip completion."

2. IMPORTANT CONTEXT CHANGE (added 30 Sep 2026)
>> LANDMARK VOICE PROMPTS ("Turn left after the Indian Oil petrol pump")
   with a louder, helmet-optimised audio profile were RELEASED to all 2W
   users in Bengaluru on 15 Sep 2026.
>> Any research findings about "can't understand / hear turn
   instructions" were collected BEFORE this release and are historical
   context only — do not base new recommendations on them without
   re-testing.

3. METHOD
- 6 in-depth interviews (45 min, remote), 2 – 12 Sep 2026
- Recruitment: users who left a Play Store review of Google Maps in the
  last 7 days and opted in to research
- Location: all participants from Bengaluru (HSR Layout, Koramangala,
  BTM, Whitefield) — chosen for speed of recruitment
- Follow-up in-app survey: 20 – 27 Sep 2026, n = 212 responses
  (2W mode users in Bengaluru)

4. DISCUSSION GUIDE (excerpt)
Q1. Walk me through the last time you used navigation on your two-wheeler.
Q2. How annoying is it when Google Maps drains your battery?
Q3. What would make you trust navigation for the whole trip?
Q4. Have you ever stopped navigation mid-trip? What happened?
Q5. Do you use any other app or method for directions?

5. CONSENT
Participants consented to recording. PII (phone numbers, full names) must
NOT appear in any shared deliverable. Use participant IDs (P1–P6) only.
