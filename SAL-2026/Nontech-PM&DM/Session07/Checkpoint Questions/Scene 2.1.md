#### **Scene 2.1**

1. **Which of these tasks is the best candidate for an AI routine?**  
   a) A confidential salary query from staff  
   b) A one-off legal dispute with a client  
   c) A rare speech to the board  
   d) A weekly status update for the team  

   **Answer:** d) A weekly status update for the team  

   **Explanation:** Frequent, repeatable, low-to-medium risk tasks repay the setup.

2. **What is a good reason to keep a task away from AI tools entirely?**  
   a) It is repetitive and follows a routine  
   b) It involves confidential or private data  
   c) It happens every single week without much variation  
   d) It follows a standard, set format  

   **Answer:** b) It involves confidential or private data  

   **Explanation:** Confidential or high-stakes work stays with you, or in an approved tool.

3. **Which part of a prompt template changes each time you use it?**  
   a) The role you assign to the AI  
   b) The rules you set for the task  
   c) The input slot with new material  
   d) The output format you ask for each time  

   **Answer:** c) The input slot with new material  

   **Explanation:** The role, rules and format stay fixed; only the pasted material changes.

4. **Why split a research task into several chained prompts?**  
   a) Each small step is much easier to check  
   b) AI tools always refuse long prompts  
   c) It always costs a lot less  
   d) It removes the need to review anything  

   **Answer:** a) Each small step is much easier to check  

   **Explanation:** Small steps let you catch a wrong detail before it spreads.

5. **A task takes 40 minutes by hand and 12 with AI. What keeps those 12 minutes trustworthy?**  
   a) Skipping the final review step to save time  
   b) A human check built into the whole routine  
   c) Writing a much longer and more detailed prompt  
   d) Switching to a different tool  

   **Answer:** b) A human check built into the whole routine  

   **Explanation:** The saving comes from the draft, not from skipping the check.
