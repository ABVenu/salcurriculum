#### **Scene 3.1**

1. **Which of these should you verify at the source before sending?**  
   a) A friendly greeting line at the start  
   b) A section heading somewhere in the draft  
   c) A direct quote from a named person  
   d) A bullet marker used in a list of points  

   **Answer:** c) A direct quote from a named person  

   **Explanation:** Quotes and citations are traced to the original, or removed.

2. **You cannot verify a statistic the AI gave you. What do you do with it?**  
   a) Cut it, or clearly mark it unconfirmed  
   b) Keep it in, because it sounds right  
   c) Round it to be safe  
   d) Ask the AI to confirm its own claim  

   **Answer:** a) Cut it, or clearly mark it unconfirmed  

   **Explanation:** If you cannot verify it, it does not go out as fact.

3. **A draft says "Customers who complain just want freebies." What kind of risk is this?**  
   a) Outdated context in the draft  
   b) A simple formatting issue  
   c) A fabricated source citation  
   d) Biased, unsupported logic  

   **Answer:** d) Biased, unsupported logic  

   **Explanation:** It is an unsupported generalisation, not a fact.

4. **An AI quotes last year's price as the current price. What risk is this?**  
   a) Biased, unsupported logic  
   b) Outdated or stale context  
   c) A brand voice problem  
   d) A formatting problem  

   **Answer:** b) Outdated or stale context  

   **Explanation:** The detail was true once but is no longer current.

5. **A draft shouts in capitals but the house style is plain. Which review pass fixes it?**  
   a) The data fidelity pass  
   b) The compliance pass  
   c) The brand voice pass  
   d) The sourcing pass  

   **Answer:** c) The brand voice pass  

   **Explanation:** Tone, punctuation and style rules belong to the brand-voice pass.
