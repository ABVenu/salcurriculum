#### **Scene 1.1**

1. **What should you keep when pasting a meeting transcript into an AI tool?**  
   a) Speaker names and their timestamps  
   b) Only the last five minutes of the meeting  
   c) Only the topic headings from the agenda  
   d) Nothing at all — delete all the names  

   **Answer:** a) Speaker names and their timestamps  

   **Explanation:** Names and timestamps let you trace any summary line back to the source.

2. **A group first says "ship to everyone," then later agrees "ship to 10% first." What goes in the final decisions list?**  
   a) Both, listed as two separate decisions  
   b) Only "ship to everyone," the first statement  
   c) Neither one, because they conflict  
   d) Only the 10% rollout, with a note on the change  

   **Answer:** d) Only the 10% rollout, with a note on the change  

   **Explanation:** The last word on a topic wins; note that the decision changed.

3. **A task was raised but nobody agreed to own it. What should the action item say for the owner?**  
   a) Assign it to the person who raised it  
   b) "No owner agreed" with a clear flag  
   c) Assign it to the team manager instead  
   d) Leave it out of the list  

   **Answer:** b) "No owner agreed" with a clear flag  

   **Explanation:** Flag the gap instead of guessing an owner.

4. **Which prompt instruction best prevents invented details in a summary?**  
   a) "Make it sound confident, polished and professional"  
   b) "Be as creative as possible with the wording"  
   c) "Use only what is actually written in the text"  
   d) "Keep it as short as you possibly can"  

   **Answer:** c) "Use only what is actually written in the text"  

   **Explanation:** A source-only rule stops the AI filling gaps with plausible details.

5. **A summary gives one task to the wrong person. Which of these checks should catch this?**  
   a) Checking the word count of the summary  
   b) Checking the tone used in the summary  
   c) Running a spell check on the whole summary  
   d) Comparing the owners with the source text  

   **Answer:** d) Comparing the owners with the source text  

   **Explanation:** Owners, dates, numbers and reversals are checked against the source text.
