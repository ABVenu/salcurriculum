#### **Scene 2.2**

1. **Which type of task profile scores best for AI help?**  
   a) Rare, high-stakes, and highly confidential  
   b) Frequent, repeatable, and fairly low risk  
   c) Rare, unique, and highly confidential  
   d) One-off, high-stakes, and urgent  

   **Answer:** b) Frequent, repeatable, and fairly low risk  

   **Explanation:** Frequency and repeatability make a template worthwhile; low risk keeps checking light.

2. **What exactly is an "input slot" in a prompt template?**  
   a) The box where the AI's reply appears  
   b) The name of the model you are using  
   c) The section holding the rules and the format  
   d) The marked blank where new material goes  

   **Answer:** d) The marked blank where new material goes  

   **Explanation:** It is where you paste the material that changes each time.

3. **You keep fixing the same mistake in your AI drafts. What should you do about it?**  
   a) Add a new rule to your saved template  
   b) Stop using AI for drafts altogether  
   c) Fix it by hand every single time  
   d) Switch to a new tool weekly  

   **Answer:** a) Add a new rule to your saved template  

   **Explanation:** Each repeated fix becomes a rule, so the template improves over time.
