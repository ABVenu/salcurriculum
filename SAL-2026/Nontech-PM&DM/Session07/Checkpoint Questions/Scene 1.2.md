#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **A transcript is too long for one single prompt. What can you do about it?**  
   a) Delete the middle section of the transcript so it fits  
   b) Paste only the start of it and ignore the rest  
   c) Summarise it section by section, then summarise the summaries  
   d) Wait until the transcript gets shorter by itself after some time  

   **Answer:** c) Summarise it section by section, then summarise the summaries  

   **Explanation:** Splitting long text into sections keeps the whole document in play.

2. **What should belong in every single action item?**  
   a) The owner, action, and deadline  
   b) The mood, topic, and length  
   c) The date, venue, and weather  
   d) The title, font, and colour  

   **Answer:** a) The owner, action, and deadline  

   **Explanation:** An action item names who does what by when.

3. **A summary says "budget approved: ₹80,000." The transcript says ₹50,000. What kind of error is this?**  
   a) A small formatting issue in how it is laid out  
   b) A clear number mismatch with the source text  
   c) A problem with the tone of the writing  
   d) Just a personal style choice  

   **Answer:** b) A clear number mismatch with the source text  

   **Explanation:** Every figure must match the source exactly.
