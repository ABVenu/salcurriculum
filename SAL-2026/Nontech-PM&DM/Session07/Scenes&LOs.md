Title: AI for Everyday Work: Streamlining Daily Tasks

Concept: Using AI for day-to-day BAU work like notes, research, and reports

Learning Objectives: 
Use AI to summarize a meeting transcript or long document into action items;Build a simple AI-assisted routine for a repetitive daily task;Explain how to fact-check and refine AI output before using it professionally

Scenes and LOs:

Scene 1: Document & Meeting Summarization to Action Items | Duration: 45 minutes
LO:1 Input raw meeting transcripts, notes, or dense reports into an AI tool to extract core themes, key decisions, and critical deadlines.
LO:2 Prompt AI to convert unstructured conversation text into formatted, owner-assigned action item lists and executive summaries.
LO:3 Evaluate AI-generated summaries against raw source text to identify missed context, misassigned owners, or omitted key details.

Scene 2: Building AI-Assisted Routines for Repetitive Daily Tasks | Duration: 45 minutes
LO:1 Audit recurring daily tasks (e.g., status reporting, email triaging, research synthesis) to identify high-leverage opportunities for AI assistance.
LO:2 Design reusable prompt templates and step-by-step AI workflows to standardise daily task execution.
LO:3 Integrate custom prompt workflows into daily routines to reduce task completion time while maintaining quality.

Scene 3: Professional Fact-Checking & Quality Control | Duration: 45 minutes
LO:1 Apply a systematic verification protocol to fact-check AI-generated facts, figures, quotes, and citations prior to professional distribution.
LO:2 Identify common AI output risks, such as subtle hallucinations, outdated context, biased logic, and improper formatting.
LO:3 Refine raw AI outputs using human judgment to ensure data fidelity, brand voice, and compliance with professional standards.
