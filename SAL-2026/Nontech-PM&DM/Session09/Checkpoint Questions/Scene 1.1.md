#### **Scene 1.1**

1. **What is an automation workflow in simple terms?**  
   a) A manual checklist that people tick off on paper  
   b) Connected steps that run automatically after a trigger  
   c) A spreadsheet listing all your tasks and their due dates  
   d) A meeting agenda for the whole team to follow each week  

   **Answer:** b) Connected steps that run automatically after a trigger  

   **Explanation:** A workflow is "when this happens, do that", with no one doing the steps by hand.

2. **Under n8n's Sustainable Use License, what would need a separate commercial agreement?**  
   a) Self-hosting it on a server for your own team  
   b) Using it for your own internal business tasks  
   c) Reselling it to clients as a hosted service  
   d) Using it for your own personal projects  

   **Answer:** c) Reselling it to clients as a hosted service  

   **Explanation:** Internal and personal use are allowed; reselling n8n as a service is not.

3. **How should n8n be described most accurately in writing?**  
   a) Fair-code: public source code, with some use limits  
   b) Public domain, free for anyone to use for anything  
   c) Fully open source, with no limits at all on any use  
   d) Closed source, with all of its code kept private  

   **Answer:** a) Fair-code: public source code, with some use limits  

   **Explanation:** Its source is public but the licence limits commercial reselling, so "fair-code" is the accurate term.

4. **Which of these tasks is the best candidate for automation?**  
   a) Deciding which lead deserves a personal call  
   b) Comforting an upset or angry customer  
   c) Negotiating a discount with a client  
   d) Logging every form entry into a shared sheet  

   **Answer:** d) Logging every form entry into a shared sheet  

   **Explanation:** It has a clear trigger, fixed rules and digital data; the others need judgement or empathy.

5. **What makes a process a good automation candidate in practice?**  
   a) It needs human judgement and empathy every time  
   b) It only happens about once or twice a year  
   c) It follows clear, fixed rules and repeats very often  
   d) It relies on paper records and handwritten forms alone  

   **Answer:** c) It follows clear, fixed rules and repeats very often  

   **Explanation:** Rule-based, digital, frequent work repays the effort of automating it.
