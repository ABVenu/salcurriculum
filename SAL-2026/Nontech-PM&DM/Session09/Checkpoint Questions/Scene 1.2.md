#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **In "when this happens, do that," what is the "this happens" part?**  
   a) The action that follows  
   b) The result at the end  
   c) The saved credential login  
   d) The trigger event itself  

   **Answer:** d) The trigger event itself  

   **Explanation:** The trigger is the event that starts the workflow.

2. **Company rules say customer data must stay on its own servers. Which option leans best?**  
   a) Any vendor-hosted tool online  
   b) Self-hosted n8n on its servers  
   c) A shared public account for everyone  
   d) Email attachments sent around  

   **Answer:** b) Self-hosted n8n on its servers  

   **Explanation:** Self-hosting keeps the data on servers the company controls.

3. **Which of these processes should stay with a person?**  
   a) Deciding which enquiry gets a personal call back  
   b) Copying form entries into a shared sheet  
   c) Sending a weekly summary email report  
   d) Posting an alert in chat when a payment fails  

   **Answer:** a) Deciding which enquiry gets a personal call back  

   **Explanation:** Judgement-heavy work stays human; the others follow fixed rules.
