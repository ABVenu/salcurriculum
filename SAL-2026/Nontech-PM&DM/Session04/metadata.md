Target Audience: Students from any background, may not necessarily be from a tech background

Session Notes Length: 480 lines to 500 lines max

title: AI for Everyday Work: Analytics & Decision-Making

objective: Using AI for data analysis and insight generation

type of session: theory/implementation mix — hands-on prompting exercises against a real dataset, no coding or paid tools required

topics be covered: Use an AI tool to summarize a dataset or dashboard into plain-language insights; Ask AI targeted questions to pull out trends from raw data; Judge when an AI-generated insight needs a human sanity check; Report creation

running case study: Groww (India's stock investing and wealth management app) — a synthetic, anonymised event dataset (1,000 users, ~23,500 events, April 1 – July 15, 2026) covering a compliance-heavy onboarding funnel (Sign Up → KYC → First Deposit) and a long-horizon engagement goal (recurring investing via SIPs). The same running example and verified numbers are reused across all three scenes so students can check the AI's arithmetic against a known-correct answer key. No specialised analytics or dashboarding tool is taught — every exercise is done entirely by prompting a general-purpose AI chat tool (ChatGPT or equivalent) with pasted or uploaded tables.

detailed subtopics to be covered:
Subtopic/Scene 1: Data Summarization & Plain-Language Insights | Duration: 40 minutes
LO:1 Upload or input structured raw data and dashboards into an AI tool to generate clear, plain-language executive summaries.
LO:2 Identify key summary elements, including overall performance metrics, macro patterns, and anomalous data points.
LO:3 Evaluate AI summary accuracy against visual data to ensure key takeaways are captured without distortion.

Subtopic/Scene 2: Trend Extraction & Targeted Querying | Duration: 45 minutes
LO:1 Formulate targeted analytical queries and prompt chains to surface hidden trends, correlations, and outliers from datasets.
LO:2 Guide AI step-by-step through exploratory data analysis (EDA) techniques to ask meaningful follow-up questions.
LO:3 Filter out statistical noise and irrelevant AI outputs to focus purely on business-critical trends.

Subtopic/Scene 3: Human Verification & AI-Assisted Report Creation | Duration: 45 minutes
LO:1 Recognize the limitations, edge cases, and potential hallucination risks of AI data analysis to determine when human sanity checks are mandatory.
LO:2 Conduct sanity checks on AI outputs by verifying calculations, checking sample sizes, and validating domain logic.
LO:3 Translate verified AI insights and trends into a structured, decision-ready final report or executive brief.

source materials adapted for this session:

- Groww event-level dataset (23,498 rows, 21 event types across 1,000 users) — aggregated into resources/*.csv for in-class pasting into an AI chat tool.
