#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **Why aggregate raw data before pasting it into AI?**  
   a) AI can't read tables over 10 rows  
   b) It removes the need to verify later  
   c) Raw rows must become images first  
   d) A small table is easier to check  

   **Answer:** d) A small table is easier to check  

   **Explanation:** Aggregation makes the prompt and the answer easier to verify — it doesn't remove the need to check.

2. **A report says only "average attendance was 85%." What's missing?**  
   a) Nothing, it's complete  
   b) The trend pattern and any unusual days  
   c) A seating chart  
   d) Next year's forecast  

   **Answer:** b) The trend pattern and any unusual days  

   **Explanation:** One overall number alone leaves out the macro pattern and any anomalies.

3. **AI says sales "tripled" (3 to 9 items). What should you check first?**  
   a) Nothing, the maths is correct so it's fine  
   b) The AI's tone  
   c) Whether the sample size is big enough to matter  
   d) The chart's logo  

   **Answer:** c) Whether the sample size is big enough to matter  

   **Explanation:** The maths is right, but 3 to 9 is too small a sample to call it a real trend.
