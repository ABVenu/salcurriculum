#### **Scene 3.2**

1. **AI says failure rate "tripled" (2 to 6, out of 50,000). What's the risk?**  
   a) None, the maths is correct  
   b) The tiny sample makes "tripled" misleading  
   c) Failure rates can't be percentages  
   d) AI must have refused  

   **Answer:** b) The tiny sample makes "tripled" misleading  

   **Explanation:** The maths is right, but tiny counts make "tripled" sound far more alarming than it is.

2. **A redesign launched the same week signups rose. What's the careful description?**  
   a) "Signups rose the same week; other causes not ruled out."  
   b) "The redesign caused the rise."  
   c) "The redesign had no effect."  
   d) "This proves redesigns always work."  

   **Answer:** a) "Signups rose the same week; other causes not ruled out."  

   **Explanation:** Happening together is correlation, not proof the redesign caused it.

3. **Which recommendation is decision-ready?**  
   a) "Do more marketing."  
   b) "Numbers went up, which is good."  
   c) "A/B test a new onboarding email for 2 weeks; measure activation rate."  
   d) "Improve the experience somehow."  

   **Answer:** c) "A/B test a new onboarding email for 2 weeks; measure activation rate."  

   **Explanation:** It names a specific, testable action and exactly how success is measured.
