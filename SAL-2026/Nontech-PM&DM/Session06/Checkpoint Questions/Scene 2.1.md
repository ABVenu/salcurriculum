#### **Scene 2.1**

1. **A campaign produces strong awareness but few users later enter consideration. What should the team investigate first?**  
   a) Whether awareness messaging creates relevance and a logical next customer action  
   b) Whether decision-stage discounts can compensate for weak mid-funnel engagement  
   c) Whether total impressions should increase before judging consideration behaviour  
   d) Whether acquisition reporting should exclude users who did not convert immediately  

   **Answer:** a) Whether awareness messaging creates relevance and a logical next customer action  

   **Explanation:** Awareness that does not become consideration should be checked for relevance and a clear next step for the customer.

2. **A SaaS campaign generates many qualified trials, but most users stop using the product within two weeks. Which metric deserves greater attention?**  
   a) Impression frequency among people who have never visited the product website  
   b) Activation and early usage behaviour among newly acquired trial customers  
   c) Click-through rate for awareness advertisements during the initial launch period  
   d) Search impression share for high-intent keywords used before the trial begins  

   **Answer:** b) Activation and early usage behaviour among newly acquired trial customers  

   **Explanation:** Trials that stop within two weeks are an activation and early-usage question, which acquisition impressions do not answer.

3. **Why should campaign messaging change between awareness and decision stages?**  
   a) Each stage represents different customer questions, information needs and perceived risks  
   b) Each stage requires a completely different brand positioning and customer segment  
   c) Decision-stage buyers respond only to price while awareness buyers respond to emotion  
   d) Awareness communication should avoid product benefits until every customer knows the brand  

   **Answer:** a) Each stage represents different customer questions, information needs and perceived risks  

   **Explanation:** The message changes because the customer's question, the information they need, and the risk they feel are different at each stage.

4. **A team launches paid media before tracking, landing pages and lead routing are fully ready. What is the main strategic risk?**  
   a) Initial demand may arrive before the system can capture, measure and process it effectively  
   b) Media platforms will permanently penalise the brand for changing campaign settings later  
   c) Awareness activity will always generate lower conversion than campaigns launched after sales  
   d) Customer acquisition cost becomes impossible to improve after the first week of campaigning  

   **Answer:** a) Initial demand may arrive before the system can capture, measure and process it effectively  

   **Explanation:** Paid media before tracking, landing pages, and lead routing means demand can arrive before the team can capture, measure, and handle it.

5. **Campaign X has lower CAC than Campaign Y but far worse repeat purchase and retention. What is the strongest conclusion?**  
   a) Campaign X is superior because acquisition efficiency should dominate campaign evaluation  
   b) Campaign Y is superior because retention should always outweigh acquisition efficiency  
   c) Neither can be judged properly without considering downstream customer economics and value  
   d) Both campaigns are equally effective because different funnel metrics cannot be compared  

   **Answer:** c) Neither can be judged properly without considering downstream customer economics and value  

   **Explanation:** A lower acquisition cost with worse repeat purchase and retention cannot be judged without the value of the customers who stay.
