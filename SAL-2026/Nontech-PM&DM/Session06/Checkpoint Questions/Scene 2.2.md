#### **Scene 2.2**

1. **Traffic to a product page increases sharply, but add-to-cart behaviour remains unchanged. Which interpretation is strongest?**  
   a) Upper-funnel activity is succeeding, so the campaign should scale without other changes  
   b) Additional visitors are arriving, but product relevance or persuasion may remain weak  
   c) Purchase intent is improving because traffic growth normally precedes cart growth  
   d) Awareness frequency is too low because product-page visits should always create cart activity  

   **Answer:** b) Additional visitors are arriving, but product relevance or persuasion may remain weak  

   **Explanation:** More product-page visits with the same add-to-cart rate means people are arriving, while relevance or persuasion is still weak.

2. **Why should a launch plan use different success metrics than ongoing marketing?**  
   a) Because the buyer is the same person so the numbers must match exactly  
   b) Because launch is time-bound first traction, while ongoing is retention and lifetime value  
   c) Because launch should use churn while ongoing never uses churn  
   d) Because ongoing marketing never needs any acquisition metrics at all  

   **Answer:** b) Because launch is time-bound first traction, while ongoing is retention and lifetime value  

   **Explanation:** Launch chases first traction; ongoing work is judged on retention and lifetime value.

3. **Which pair best contrasts launch metrics with ongoing metrics?**  
   a) Launch: vanity impressions only. Ongoing: vanity impressions on another app.  
   b) Launch: total ad spend only. Ongoing: total ad spend in email only.  
   c) Launch: day-one signups and press coverage. Ongoing: repeat purchase rate, retention, and churn.  
   d) Launch: follower count only. Ongoing: follower count in a second city.  

   **Answer:** c) Launch: day-one signups and press coverage. Ongoing: repeat purchase rate, retention, and churn.  

   **Explanation:** Launch tracks the opening spike; ongoing tracks whether the customer relationship pays off.
