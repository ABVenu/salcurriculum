#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **A premium B2B SaaS company receives many Google leads but sales says most buyers are too small. What should the marketer investigate first?**  
   a) Whether keyword intent and qualification criteria match the intended segment  
   b) Whether social reach is large enough to compensate for lead-quality weakness  
   c) Whether the sales team can contact every lead within a shorter response window  
   d) Whether lowering the product price would make existing leads financially viable  

   **Answer:** a) Whether keyword intent and qualification criteria match the intended segment  

   **Explanation:** Leads that are too small for the intended segment point first to keyword intent and the qualification criteria.

2. **A brand's target customers spend significant time on Instagram but rarely search for its unfamiliar product category. What is the strongest initial role for Instagram?**  
   a) Capture existing bottom-funnel intent from buyers comparing specific suppliers  
   b) Generate problem awareness and create interest before active search develops  
   c) Replace all performance channels because audience presence proves purchase intent  
   d) Handle product qualification through long-form personalised sales conversations  

   **Answer:** b) Generate problem awareness and create interest before active search develops  

   **Explanation:** When the category is unfamiliar and people are not searching, Instagram's first job is to create the problem and the interest.

3. **Which GTM snapshot is strategically strongest?**  
   a) One listing channels, budgets and creative formats without customer assumptions  
   b) One documenting audience, proposition, channel roles, actions and success measures  
   c) One containing every possible channel so the team avoids missing opportunities  
   d) One focusing entirely on revenue targets without intermediate success indicators  

   **Answer:** b) One documenting audience, proposition, channel roles, actions and success measures  

   **Explanation:** A strong snapshot records the audience, the proposition, each channel's role, the actions, and how success is measured.
