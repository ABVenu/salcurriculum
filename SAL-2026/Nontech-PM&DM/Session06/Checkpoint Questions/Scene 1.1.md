#### **Scene 1.1**

1. **A new software category solves a problem that most buyers have not yet recognised. Which GTM approach is strongest initially?**  
   a) Prioritise high-intent search terms and minimise educational activity  
   b) Build category awareness while creating routes to capture emerging intent  
   c) Focus on retargeting users who have already reached the pricing page  
   d) Shift spending toward branded search before broadening communication  

   **Answer:** b) Build category awareness while creating routes to capture emerging intent  

   **Explanation:** Buyers who do not yet recognise the problem need category awareness, and a path ready for intent once it appears.

2. **A company sells ₹40 lakh cybersecurity contracts to a defined list of 600 enterprises. Which channel logic is most appropriate?**  
   a) Maximise broad consumer reach before introducing direct seller engagement  
   b) Prioritise mass social reach because larger audiences reduce acquisition risk  
   c) Combine targeted media, account content and personalised sales outreach  
   d) Use only search because active intent eliminates the need for nurturing  

   **Answer:** c) Combine targeted media, account content and personalised sales outreach  

   **Explanation:** A defined list of 600 enterprises buying ₹40 lakh contracts is reached through targeted media, account content, and personal outreach.

3. **A campaign receives strong social engagement but very few qualified buyers. Which diagnosis should be tested first?**  
   a) The channel may generate attention without sufficiently matching buying intent  
   b) The campaign necessarily requires more reach before conversions can emerge  
   c) The brand should remove qualification criteria to increase downstream volume  
   d) The same creative should be transferred unchanged to high-intent channels  

   **Answer:** a) The channel may generate attention without sufficiently matching buying intent  

   **Explanation:** Strong engagement with few qualified buyers is a sign the channel is creating attention that does not match buying intent.

4. **When assessing whether WhatsApp belongs in a GTM plan, which factor is most strategically relevant?**  
   a) Whether competitors currently use WhatsApp as part of their marketing mix  
   b) Whether the audience permits direct contact and the interaction adds value  
   c) Whether WhatsApp offers lower CPM than every available advertising platform  
   d) Whether the brand can replace email and sales calls entirely with messaging  

   **Answer:** b) Whether the audience permits direct contact and the interaction adds value  

   **Explanation:** WhatsApp belongs when the audience accepts direct contact and the interaction itself adds value.

5. **What is the strongest purpose of asking AI to critique an AI-generated GTM snapshot?**  
   a) To obtain a second output without requiring additional customer information  
   b) To replace channel testing with a more detailed strategic recommendation  
   c) To expose assumptions, contradictions and gaps before resources are committed  
   d) To ensure the final GTM plan contains every possible marketing channel  

   **Answer:** c) To expose assumptions, contradictions and gaps before resources are committed  

   **Explanation:** The critique is meant to surface assumptions, contradictions, and gaps before money and effort are committed.
