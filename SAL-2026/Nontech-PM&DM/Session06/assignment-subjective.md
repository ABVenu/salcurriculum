# Assignment Subjective

Difficulty: Easy

Questions: 1 short written task

---

## Task: Six short lines for DeskBite

### Problem

DeskBite is a baked snack sold online for **₹60**.

The buyers are office workers aged **25–40**. At 4 p.m. they eat fried namkeen. Most of them are **not searching** for DeskBite.

Write six short answers. One or two sentences each is enough.

1. Is the first job **capture** or **generate**? Say why in one sentence.
2. Name **one channel** to generate demand and **one channel** to capture it.
3. Write **one awareness line**. Mention the 4 p.m. problem only. Do not add a buy link.
4. Name **one metric** for awareness and **one metric** for retention.
5. Campaign A costs **₹500** to acquire a customer, brings **10,000** customers, and **70%** never buy again. Campaign B costs **₹700**, brings **8,000** customers, and **60%** buy again. Which one would you rather scale? One sentence on why.
6. Write the five words in the channel test **A-I-C-V-S**.

### Submission instruction

- Type the answer in the answer box.

---

## Answer Explanation

### A complete answer

1. **Generate.** Most of these workers are not searching for DeskBite, so the first job is to create the want. Capture comes later, when some of them look.
2. **Generate:** Instagram, or a short creator video of the 4 p.m. snack moment. **Capture:** Google Search, or a marketplace, for people who then look for a high-protein snack.
3. Any one line that names the 4 p.m. hunger and does not ask for a checkout. Example: "4 p.m. hunger hits, and the fried snack is already on the desk."
4. **Awareness:** reach, or brand search, or video completion. **Retention:** repeat purchase, or churn, or lifetime value. One of each is enough.
5. **Campaign B.** A is cheaper on the first sale, but most of those customers never return. B’s repeat buyers can be worth more over time.
6. **Audience, Intent, Complexity, Value, Scale.**

### Alternative approaches

- For line 2, email or WhatsApp can be named as a later reorder channel. They are a weak first choice for people who have never heard of DeskBite.
- For line 5, "I need lifetime value before I choose" is also acceptable, as long as the answer does not pick A only because ₹500 is lower.

An answer that starts with capture, puts a "buy now" link in the awareness line, picks Campaign A only because the first sale is cheaper, or leaves out Value or Scale does not meet the task.
