detailed subtopics to be covered:
Subtopic/Scene 1: Channel Selection and the One-Page GTM Snapshot | Duration: 70 minutes
LO:1 Select primary marketing channels (social, email, search, or direct outreach) based on product type and audience behavior, using AI to research how that audience uses each channel.
LO:2 Draft a one-page GTM snapshot with AI that records the segment and positioning already set, the channel mix, the message adapted to each channel format, the key activities, and a metrics section.
LO:3 Check the AI-drafted snapshot for logical consistency and feasibility, and revise it where the plan would not hold under real constraints.

Subtopic/Scene 2: Launch Plan versus Ongoing Marketing Plan | Duration: 60 minutes
LO:1 Explain how a time-bound launch plan, aimed at first awareness and traction, differs from an ongoing marketing plan aimed at retention and growth.
LO:2 Design the sequence that shifts effort from launch momentum to sustained acquisition and retention.
LO:3 Define separate success metrics for the launch (for example, day-one signups and press coverage) and for ongoing marketing (for example, customer lifetime value and churn).
