title: Marketing Your Product - Part 1: Positioning & Messaging

objective: Crafting positioning and core messaging with AI assistance

type of session: theory/implementation mix — a running product example, then hands-on prompting to write and stress-test positioning and marketing copy

topics be covered: Identify the target audience segment and primary pain point, and state a differentiated core value proposition; Write a one-line positioning statement using a standard positioning framework; Use AI to stress-test that positioning and to generate, evaluate, and refine marketing copy

running example: FitFataak, a baked, high-protein Indian snack sold online. The same product is used across both scenes so audience, value proposition, positioning, and copy stay connected. Source deck kept in this folder: Defining-Your-Customer-Before-Your-Customer-Defines-You.pdf

detailed subtopics to be covered:
Subtopic/Scene 1: Audience, Value Proposition, and One-Line Positioning | Duration: 70 minutes
LO:1 Identify the specific target audience segment and articulate their primary pain point.
LO:2 Formulate a differentiated core value proposition, then write a one-line positioning statement in the form: For [target market] who [need], [product] is a [category] that [key benefit], so the product stands apart from existing alternatives.
LO:3 Use AI to analyze customer profiles, check that the value proposition is clear, and stress-test the positioning statement for clarity, uniqueness, and fit with the target market.

Subtopic/Scene 2: Generating and Refining Marketing Copy with AI | Duration: 60 minutes
LO:1 Use AI to generate varied taglines, landing-page headlines, and short value lines from the positioning statement.
LO:2 Evaluate the AI-generated copy for tone, clarity, and resonance with the target audience.
LO:3 Refine the copy by iterating prompts and applying human judgment so the final lines stay aligned with the brand and the positioning.
