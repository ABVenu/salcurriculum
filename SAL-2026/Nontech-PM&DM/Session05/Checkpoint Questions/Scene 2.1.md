#### **Scene 2.1**

1. **Why does STP output improve AI-generated marketing copy?**  
   a) Because AI requires demographic information to function  
   b) Because STP gives AI context about the audience, need, and positioning  
   c) Because STP automatically generates headlines  
   d) Because AI cannot create copy without segmentation  

   **Answer:** b) Because STP gives AI context about the audience, need, and positioning  

   **Explanation:** The audience, the need, and the positioning give the model a brief. STP does not write the headline by itself.

2. **Which prompt is most likely to generate useful copy?**  
   a) Write a tagline.  
   b) Write a cool tagline for a snack.  
   c) Write 10 short taglines for a protein-rich Indian snack aimed at busy office workers who get hungry around 4 PM. Keep the tone witty and avoid generic health language.  
   d) Give me marketing ideas.  

   **Answer:** c) Write 10 short taglines for a protein-rich Indian snack aimed at busy office workers who get hungry around 4 PM. Keep the tone witty and avoid generic health language.  

   **Explanation:** The useful prompt names the product, the audience, the occasion, the count, the tone, and what to avoid.

3. **The headline "Transforming nutrition through intelligent snack innovation" — what is its biggest weakness?**  
   a) It is too short  
   b) It may lack clarity and sound generic — any competitor could say it  
   c) It contains the word "nutrition"  
   d) It doesn't contain a discount  

   **Answer:** b) It may lack clarity and sound generic — any competitor could say it  

   **Explanation:** The line has no customer, no occasion, and no claim a rival could not make.

4. **Which line demonstrates the strongest audience resonance?**  
   a) We use high-quality ingredients.  
   b) 15 grams of protein per pack.  
   c) Stay full through the meeting that somehow got scheduled at 5 PM.  
   d) Available in multiple flavours.  

   **Answer:** c) Stay full through the meeting that somehow got scheduled at 5 PM.  

   **Explanation:** Resonance places the benefit in the customer's situation. Ingredients, grams, and flavours stay at feature level.

5. **Which is the best role for AI in copywriting?**  
   a) Produce final copy and publish it without review  
   b) Replace customer research entirely  
   c) Generate, critique, and refine options while humans provide strategic judgment  
   d) Independently decide brand positioning  

   **Answer:** c) Generate, critique, and refine options while humans provide strategic judgment  

   **Explanation:** AI drafts and critiques options. Publishing, research, and positioning stay with the human.
