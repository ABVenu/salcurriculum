#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **A startup defines its audience as "Men and women aged 18–45 interested in fitness." What is the biggest problem?**  
   a) Demographics should never be used  
   b) The audience is probably too broad to reveal a specific customer need or behaviour  
   c) Fitness cannot be used for segmentation  
   d) Target audiences should never include age  

   **Answer:** b) The audience is probably too broad to reveal a specific customer need or behaviour  

   **Explanation:** The range is so wide that it does not reveal one need or behaviour to build the offer around.

2. **A company says: "Our software uses an advanced proprietary machine-learning algorithm." Which rewrite gets closest to customer value?**  
   a) Our algorithm includes 14 machine-learning models  
   b) We are India's most innovative technology company  
   c) Our software identifies high-priority leads so sales teams spend less time chasing low-intent prospects  
   d) We use cutting-edge technology  

   **Answer:** c) Our software identifies high-priority leads so sales teams spend less time chasing low-intent prospects  

   **Explanation:** Customer value is the outcome for the user. Model counts, innovation claims, and "cutting-edge" stay at feature or slogan level.

3. **You ask AI to create a customer persona. What should happen next?**  
   a) Assume it represents the market  
   b) Immediately build the campaign around it  
   c) Validate its assumptions using real customer and behavioural data  
   d) Ask AI to create a logo for the persona  

   **Answer:** c) Validate its assumptions using real customer and behavioural data  

   **Explanation:** An AI persona is a hypothesis. Real customer and behavioural data have to confirm it.
