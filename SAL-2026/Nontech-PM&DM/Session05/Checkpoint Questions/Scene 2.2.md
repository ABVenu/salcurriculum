#### **Scene 2.2**

1. **AI produces: "Unlock Seamless Nutrition for Modern Living." What should you do first?**  
   a) Publish it because it sounds professional  
   b) Add more sophisticated words  
   c) Check whether it is clear, specific and relevant to the target audience  
   d) Ask AI to make it longer  

   **Answer:** c) Check whether it is clear, specific and relevant to the target audience  

   **Explanation:** The first check is clarity, specificity, and relevance. Publishing, puff words, or extra length do not fix a generic line.

2. **Which is the strongest refinement of "Our snack contains 15g protein" for an office-working audience?**  
   a) Our product leverages advanced protein nutrition.  
   b) 15g protein to help you stay full through the afternoon.  
   c) Protein is an important macronutrient.  
   d) We proudly provide nutritional innovation.  

   **Answer:** b) 15g protein to help you stay full through the afternoon.  

   **Explanation:** The revision keeps the proof and ties it to the office afternoon. The other lines add jargon without a customer outcome.

3. **Why is human judgment still important after AI has generated and evaluated copy?**  
   a) Humans type faster  
   b) AI cannot generate headlines  
   c) Humans bring brand context, cultural understanding, strategy and real-world customer insight  
   d) AI can only write social-media captions  

   **Answer:** c) Humans bring brand context, cultural understanding, strategy and real-world customer insight  

   **Explanation:** AI can draft and critique lines. Brand context, culture, strategy, and customer insight still need a person.
