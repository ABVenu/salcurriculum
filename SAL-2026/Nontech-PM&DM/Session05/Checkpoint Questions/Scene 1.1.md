#### **Scene 1.1**

1. **What is the primary purpose of segmentation?**  
   a) To create as many customer groups as possible  
   b) To divide a market into groups with meaningfully different needs or behaviours  
   c) To reduce the price of the product  
   d) To select advertising platforms  

   **Answer:** b) To divide a market into groups with meaningfully different needs or behaviours  

   **Explanation:** Segmentation splits a market into groups whose needs or behaviours actually differ.

2. **Which is an example of behavioural segmentation?**  
   a) Customers aged 25–35  
   b) Customers living in Delhi and Mumbai  
   c) Customers who order food after 10 PM at least twice a week  
   d) Customers earning more than ₹10 lakh annually  

   **Answer:** c) Customers who order food after 10 PM at least twice a week  

   **Explanation:** Behavioural segmentation uses what people do. Age, city, and income are demographic or geographic.

3. **A company identifies four customer groups but decides to primarily serve working professionals. Which STP stage is this?**  
   a) Segmentation  
   b) Targeting  
   c) Positioning  
   d) Personalisation  

   **Answer:** b) Targeting  

   **Explanation:** Choosing which of the groups to serve is targeting. Segmentation only creates the groups.

4. **Which is the strongest example of a customer insight?**  
   a) Customers are aged 25–40  
   b) Customers live in metro cities  
   c) Customers often get hungry around 4 PM but avoid regular snacks because they perceive them as unhealthy  
   d) Customers own smartphones  

   **Answer:** c) Customers often get hungry around 4 PM but avoid regular snacks because they perceive them as unhealthy  

   **Explanation:** An insight states the situation and the reason. Age, city, and device ownership do not.

5. **Which statement best describes a Point of Difference?**  
   a) A feature every competitor must offer  
   b) Something that matters to customers and helps distinguish the brand  
   c) The company's annual revenue  
   d) A demographic characteristic of the audience  

   **Answer:** b) Something that matters to customers and helps distinguish the brand  

   **Explanation:** A point of difference is what helps the customer choose this brand. A feature everyone must offer is a point of parity.
