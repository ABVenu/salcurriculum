#### **Scene 2.2**

1. **Which headline follows the rule for a first landing page?**  
   a) Welcome to our website  
   b) Welcome to platform version one  
   c) A short line that names the offer or the outcome  
   d) A paragraph of company history before any offer appears  

   **Answer:** c) A short line that names the offer or the outcome  

   **Explanation:** The headline is the first line a visitor reads. It should name the offer or outcome, not a generic welcome or a history essay.

2. **After a chat tool drafts landing-page copy, what should you do first?**  
   a) Edit jargon and delete numbers or claims you did not supply  
   b) Publish the draft unchanged so the page stays fully automatic  
   c) Add fake ratings so the page looks already trusted  
   d) Replace the single button with login, shop, and blog links  

   **Answer:** a) Edit jargon and delete numbers or claims you did not supply  

   **Explanation:** AI drafts words; you remain the editor. Invented counts, testimonials, and extra buttons should be removed before the page is shown.

3. **What is a visual direction for a first prototype?**  
   a) The instruction make it look premium, with no subject or setting  
   b) A plain brief of subject, setting, and what to avoid in the picture  
   c) A claim that a generated person in the picture is a real customer  
   d) A requirement to fill the page with as many stock photos as possible  

   **Answer:** b) A plain brief of subject, setting, and what to avoid in the picture  

   **Explanation:** Visuals should explain, not decorate. A useful brief names what to show and what not to show. Generated pictures are placeholders, not proof of real customers.
