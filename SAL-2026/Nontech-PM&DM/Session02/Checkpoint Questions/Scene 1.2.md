#### **Scene 1.2**

1. **After you pick one idea, what should a refine step make clear?**  
   a) Payments, ratings, and a map so the idea looks already complete  
   b) The user, the problem, the first version, and what to leave out  
   c) Invented survey percentages so the idea sounds already proven  
   d) A request that the model praise the idea and hide any remaining risk  

   **Answer:** b) The user, the problem, the first version, and what to leave out  

   **Explanation:** Refine narrows one winner. Naming what the first version must not do stops a feature pile. Invented numbers and praise are not a refine.

2. **Which target user is specific enough to stress-test?**  
   a) Anyone in the country who owns a mobile phone  
   b) Students in general, in any city, at any hour  
   c) One kind of person, in a real place, at the moment of pain  
   d) People who will appear only after a huge advertising campaign  

   **Answer:** c) One kind of person, in a real place, at the moment of pain  

   **Explanation:** “Everyone” and “students” are too wide. A stress-test needs a person you could actually meet, plus when the pain happens.

3. **Why should you reject a value line packed with words such as ecosystem and revolutionary?**  
   a) Those words are required on every professional landing page  
   b) Models refuse to generate any sentence that omits those words  
   c) A classmate can always repeat a jargon line after hearing it once  
   d) They hide the user, the job, and the pain the offer should name  

   **Answer:** d) They hide the user, the job, and the pain the offer should name  

   **Explanation:** Jargon is not a value proposition. If a classmate cannot repeat the line, it is still too vague or too decorated.
